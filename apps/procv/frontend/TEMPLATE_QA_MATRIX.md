# Template QA Matrix

Date: 2026-03-16
Scope: Visual QA and product readiness review for the 9 curated CV templates.

## Scoring rubric (1-10)
- Visual hierarchy: title/section/read flow clarity
- Density balance: information compactness vs breathing room
- ATS readiness: machine-readable structure tendency
- Brand differentiation: distinct look without redundancy
- Overall premium feel: commercial quality perception

## Remaining opportunities
